# Security Policy

NO-DO builds security and OSINT tooling (TRACEZ, GRIMZ, DEEPWIRE) and publishes
independent threat research. If you find a vulnerability in a NO-DO product or an
error in published research, please report it privately first.

## Reporting a vulnerability

- Email: contacto@no-do.dev
- Please include the affected asset or URL, the impact, and clear reproduction steps.
- Expect acknowledgement within 72 hours. We will agree a coordinated-disclosure
  timeline with you before anything is published.

## Scope

- **In scope:** NO-DO web properties under `*.no-do.dev` and research in this
  account's public repositories.
- **Out of scope:** third-party infrastructure, volumetric denial-of-service,
  social engineering, and physical attacks.

## Public research

Threat research in this account ships at **TLP:CLEAR** for defenders. Corrections
and additional sightings are welcome as issues on the relevant repository.
